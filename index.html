Heaven Simulator

A gentle, nondenominational journey through the elements people most often describe after near-death experiences, as recorded in clinical research and published testimony.

The border

A river runs here that you sense you cannot cross yet. Many people describe a boundary like this, where they chose or were told to return (paraphrase).

Based on testimonies

Each scene follows elements that come up again and again in near-death research:

  • Out of body: seeing your body from above (24% in van Lommel 2001; Greyson NDE Scale item 12)
  • Peace, joy: positive emotions (56%; Greyson items 5–6)
  • Tunnel: moving through a tunnel toward light (31%; Moody 1975)
  • Being of Light: a presence of love (23% "communication with light"; Moody)
  • Vivid colours, unearthly landscape: (23% / 29%; Greyson items 9, 13)
  • Deceased loved ones: (32%; Greyson item 15)
  • Life review: (13%; Greyson item 3; Moody)
  • Border, return: (8%; Greyson item 16; Moody)

Sources: van Lommel et al., Lancet 2001 · Greyson NDE Scale · Greyson on Moody's 15 features · Greyson overview (PMC) · IANDS · NDERF

All on-screen text paraphrases these reports. Nothing here is a direct quote. This is an artistic interpretation and makes no claim about what happens after death.
